# Hi, I'm Anastasios 👋

I'm a streaming and OTT engineer based in Athens, Greece. Since 2021 I've been working on **ERTFLIX**, the streaming platform of the Hellenic Broadcasting Corporation (ERT), where I look after the systems that get content from the broadcast side of the building onto people's screens.

Most of what I do sits in between broadcast infrastructure and web engineering: live encoding and packaging on one side, APIs and automation on the other.

## What I work on

**Content pipeline** — I designed and built the content management and distribution system that bridges ERT's broadcast MAM systems and the OTT backend. It handles the full asset lifecycle: ingest, metadata, automated publishing, and storage cleanup. It's the editorial team's only path for getting content onto the platform, so it runs every day and it has to work.

**Live streaming** — Live event configuration and execution with AWS Elemental and Wowza Streaming Engine, plus production monitoring and real-time troubleshooting. Also manage origin and packaging (Broadpeak, Ateme) and the CDN side.

**Integrations & automation** — An API integration with EBU Pilot that syndicates ERT news articles the moment they're published, a daily job that generates content feeds for distribution partners, and an internal tool that lets the broadcast team switch between live air and a video loop on the fly.

**Client apps** — Debugging and testing across Android, Android TV, LG TV, Samsung TV, and HbbTV. Smart TV platforms are their own special kind of fun.
