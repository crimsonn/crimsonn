# Hi, I'm Anastasios 👋
 
I'm a streaming and OTT engineer based in Athens, Greece. Since 2021 I've been working on **[ERTFLIX](https://www.ertflix.gr)**, the streaming platform of the Hellenic Broadcasting Corporation (ERT), where I look after the systems that get content from the broadcast side of the building onto people's screens.
 
Most of what I do sits in between broadcast infrastructure and web engineering: live encoding and packaging on one side, APIs and automation on the other.
 
## What I work on
 
**Content pipeline** — I designed and built the content management and distribution system that bridges ERT's broadcast MAM systems and the OTT backend. It handles the full asset lifecycle: ingest, metadata, automated publishing, and storage cleanup. It's the editorial team's only path for getting content onto the platform, so it runs every day and it has to work.
 
**Live streaming** — I configure and run live events on AWS Elemental and Wowza Streaming Engine, and handle production monitoring and real-time troubleshooting when things break on air. I also manage origin and packaging (Broadpeak, Ateme) and the CDN side.
 
**Integrations & automation** — I built an API integration with EBU Pilot that syndicates ERT news articles the moment they're published, a daily job that generates content feeds for distribution partners, and an internal tool that lets the broadcast team switch between live air and a video loop on the fly.
 
**Playback across devices** — I test and debug playback on Android, Android TV, LG TV, Samsung TV, and HbbTV. Smart TV platforms are their own special kind of fun.
